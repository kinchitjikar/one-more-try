Privacy Policy for PulseTap: One More Try
Last Updated: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction
PulseTap: One More Try ("we", "our", or "the Game") is a reflex arcade mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our application.

2. Information Collection and Storage
• Offline-First Design: Game progress, high scores, streaks, levels, and user settings (sound/haptics toggles) are stored locally on your device via secure on-device storage. We do not operate custom backend servers and do not maintain accounts, passwords, or personal profiles.
• No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations.

3. Advertising & Analytics
The Game uses Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC) to serve advertisements:
• Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), device information, and non-personal usage telemetry to deliver contextual or personalized advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
• Rewarded video ads are strictly optional and only triggered when you explicitly choose to watch an ad to revive your game session.

4. Children’s Privacy
PulseTap: One More Try does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The application is designed to be family-safe and suitable for general audiences.

5. Data Security
Because all your game data is stored directly on your device, you can delete all local data at any time by clearing the application cache/data in Android Settings or using the "Reset All Stats" option in the in-game Settings menu.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this document with an updated "Last Updated" date.
