## 2. Full Description (Google Play Store compliant)
Can your reflexes survive the pulse?

PulseTap: One More Try is a fast-paced, minimalist reflex arcade game engineered to test your reaction speed, focus, and precision. Built for players who crave instant action and high-stakes arcade competition, every millisecond counts. 

The objective is simple:
Tap the glowing crimson target before the pulse depletes.
Avoid the deceptive amber decoys.
Build your streak, unlock higher levels, and prove your reaction time.
One mistake, and it’s game over. Do you have what it takes for ONE MORE TRY?

⚡ HOW TO PLAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. TAP THE TARGET: Tap the pulsing neon target before the countdown timer runs out.
2. WATCH THE TIMER: Each target has a lifetime counter. Tap fast for massive Speed Bonuses!
3. DODGE DECOYS: As the level scales, treacherous amber decoys appear. Never tap them!
4. BUILD MULTIPLIERS: Chain consecutive successful taps to build your streak multiplier.
5. SURVIVE OR REVIVE: One mistake ends your run. Retry instantly or watch a quick rewarded video to keep your streak alive.
